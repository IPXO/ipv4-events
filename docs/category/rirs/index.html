Regional Internet Registries — IPv4 Era Timeline

The five Regional Internet Registries (RIRs) — ARIN, RIPE NCC, APNIC, LACNIC, and AFRINIC — manage the allocation of IPv4 and IPv6 address space across the globe. This section tracks their founding, major policy milestones, and the exhaustion of the IPv4 free pool.

IPv4 exhaustion events are central to the RIR timeline: APNIC's free pool ran out in 2011, RIPE NCC in 2012, ARIN in 2015, LACNIC in 2014, and AFRINIC in 2021 — marking the true end of the IPv4 era's expansion phase.
