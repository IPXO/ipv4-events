1980s Technology Milestones — IPv4 Era Timeline

The 1980s were the decade TCP/IP took over. On January 1, 1983 — "Flag Day" — ARPANET switched to TCP/IP. The DNS system was formalized (1983), the first commercial ISPs emerged, and the internet began its transition from military research network to global public infrastructure.

The 1980s also saw the IBM PC revolution, the rise of Unix workstations, the establishment of the first RIRs, and the Morris Worm (1988) — the first major internet security incident, demonstrating the vulnerabilities of a connected network.
